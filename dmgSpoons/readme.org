* dmgSpoons

Spoons written for one machine. They name particular devices, accounts or files, and would
have to be rewritten before they were of use to anyone else. Nothing here is meant to be
published as it stands.

Spoons meant to be used by anyone are in =../Spoons/=.

* What is here

| Spoon                            | Does                                                        |
|----------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------|
| [[file:hs_hass-gt/][hs_hass-gt]] | Home Assistant control: lights, scripts and the desk amplifiers, through =hass-cli=. Names the entities in one house. |
| [[file:hs_url-gt/][hs_url-gt]]   | Routes every link opened on the system, by a table of patterns or by handing it to Emacs =browse-url=. Names particular sites and browsers. |
